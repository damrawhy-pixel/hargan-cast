## English

**App name** (30)
Hargan Cast

**Short description** (80)
Play PlayStation and Xbox cloud games from one app, with a console-like feel.

**Full description** (4000)
Hargan Cast brings PlayStation and Xbox cloud gaming together in one app. Sign in with your own accounts, browse your library and stream games on your phone or tablet.

A CONSOLE-STYLE HOME
• Games from both accounts on one home screen
• Description, screenshots and player rating when you rest on a game
• Fully navigable with a controller

PLAYSTATION
• PS5, PS4 and PS3 games from the PS Plus Premium catalog
• Games you own that can be streamed
• Up to 1080p, with H.265 support

XBOX
• Xbox Cloud Gaming titles and cloud games you own
• Queue position and time left while you wait

PICTURE AND CONTROLS
• Upscaling and debanding that sharpen the picture for your screen
• DualSense, Xbox and other Bluetooth/USB controllers
• On-screen touch controls when you have no controller: two sticks, D-pad and triggers, with adjustable size and opacity
• The screen stays on and Wi-Fi switches to low-latency mode while streaming

PRIVACY
Hargan Cast has no servers of its own. The app connects only to Microsoft and Sony, collects no data, and has no ads or tracking. Your account details stay on your device.

REQUIREMENTS
• For PlayStation: an account with a PS Plus Premium subscription
• For Xbox: an Xbox Game Pass subscription that includes cloud gaming
• A stable, fast internet connection (5 GHz Wi-Fi recommended)
