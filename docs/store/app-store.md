## English

**Name** (30)
Hargan Cast

**Subtitle** (30)
PlayStation & Xbox cloud play

**Promotional text** (170)
Your PlayStation and Xbox cloud games on one home screen. Play with a controller or the on-screen controls, with a console-like feel.

**Description** (4000)
Hargan Cast brings PlayStation and Xbox cloud gaming together in one app. Sign in with your own accounts, browse your library and stream games on your iPhone or iPad.

A CONSOLE-STYLE HOME
• Games from both accounts on one home screen
• Description, screenshots and player rating when you rest on a game
• Fully navigable with a controller

PLAYSTATION
• PS5, PS4 and PS3 games from the PS Plus Premium catalog
• Games you own that can be streamed

XBOX
• Xbox Cloud Gaming titles and cloud games you own
• Queue position and time left while you wait

PICTURE AND CONTROLS
• Upscaling and debanding that sharpen the picture for your screen
• DualSense, Xbox and other MFi controllers
• On-screen touch controls when you have no controller, with adjustable size and opacity

PRIVACY
Hargan Cast has no servers of its own. The app connects only to Microsoft and Sony, collects no data, and has no ads or tracking.

REQUIREMENTS
• For PlayStation: an account with a PS Plus Premium subscription
• For Xbox: an Xbox Game Pass subscription that includes cloud gaming
• iOS 16.4 or later and a stable, fast internet connection

Hargan Cast is open source (AGPL-3.0). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony Interactive Entertainment or Microsoft. PlayStation and Xbox are trademarks of their respective owners.

- **Yaş derecelendirmesi:** Uygulamanın kendisinde içerik yok; kullanıcı kendi hesabındaki oyunları akışla oynadığı için "Sınırsız web erişimi" ve oyun içerikleri sorularını dürüstçe yanıtla.

- **İnceleme notu (App Review):** "Uygulama, kullanıcının kendi Xbox ve PlayStation hesaplarıyla bulut oyunlarını akışla oynar. İnceleme için Game Pass ve PS Plus Premium içeren bir test hesabı gerekir." Mümkünse inceleme ekibine bir test hesabı ver.
